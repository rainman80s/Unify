Unify.js

One workspace for many AIs. When Claude Code hits its limit, Codex picks up exactly where it left off, and the other way round.

Download for Mac
Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 11 or later · Free
Intel Mac? Download the Intel version. Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip: Apple M…” means Apple Silicon.

Install in a minute

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Unify.js into Applications.
  2. First open only: macOS blocks it because it isn't from the App Store or signed by a registered Apple developer yet. Double-click it, choose Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. The Get started screen installs Claude Code and Codex if you need them, signs you in with your own accounts, and checks everything works.

What you need

  • A Claude plan (for Claude Code) and/or a ChatGPT plan (for Codex). One works; both is best.
  • Optional: a free NVIDIA API key as a backup when your plans are resting.

Your accounts stay yours

Unify.js runs on your Mac and drives the official Claude Code and Codex apps, signed in by you. It never sees your passwords, and your projects stay on your computer.
